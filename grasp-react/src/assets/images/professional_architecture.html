GIMS Task Registry System

AI-Powered WhatsApp Task Management in Marathi, Hindi & English

What is This System?

A smart digital solution that allows your supervisors to register daily maintenance tasks simply by sending WhatsApp messages in their preferred language (Marathi, Hindi, or English). The system uses Artificial Intelligence to automatically understand what they're saying, organize it into the correct category, and make it visible to management in real-time through a dashboard.

Simple Process: Supervisor speaks/types in WhatsApp → AI understands and organizes → Task appears in dashboard → Reports generated automatically

How Does It Work?

Step 1: Supervisor Communicates
Supervisors send messages via WhatsApp - either voice messages in Marathi/Hindi/English or text messages. No special apps to download, no complex forms to fill. Just natural conversation.
↓
Step 2: AI Understands
Our AI system reads or listens to the message, understands what is being said regardless of language, and extracts important information like location, person names, dates, priority levels, and task details.
↓
Step 3: Automatic Classification
The AI automatically determines which of your 9 registry categories this task belongs to (Site Visit, Email Response, Customer Communication, etc.) and organizes it accordingly.
↓
Step 4: Confirmation & Storage
The system confirms with the supervisor in their own language that the task has been registered, assigns a unique tracking number, and stores everything in a secure digital registry.
↓
Step 5: Dashboard & Reports
Management can see all tasks instantly on a web dashboard. Daily, weekly, and monthly reports are automatically generated and can be downloaded as PDF or Excel files - just like your paper register format.

Complete Message Journey

1
Supervisor Sends Message
A supervisor at a site needs to register their activity. They open WhatsApp and either send a voice message in Marathi/Hindi or type a text message in any language they're comfortable with.
2
Message Received
Our system instantly receives the WhatsApp message. If it's a voice message, the audio is saved. If it's text, the message content is captured.
3
AI Analysis
The AI engine analyzes the message. For voice, it first converts speech to text in the original language. Then it identifies what language is being used, what the task is about, where it's happening, who's involved, when it needs to be done, and how urgent it is.
4
Smart Classification
Based on the content, the AI automatically determines which of your 9 registry categories this task belongs to - whether it's a site visit, email response, customer communication, travel record, etc.
5
Clarification (if needed)
If any important information is missing (like a location or date), the system asks the supervisor a quick follow-up question in their language to get the complete details.
6
Digital Registry Entry
Once complete, the task is saved in the digital registry with a unique ID number, timestamp, category assignment, and all details properly organized. The original voice recording is also saved if it was a voice message.
7
Instant Confirmation
The supervisor immediately receives a WhatsApp confirmation message in their language showing what was registered, which category it went into, and the tracking number for reference.
8
Live Dashboard Update
At the same moment, the task appears on the management dashboard. Managers can see it in real-time under the correct category, filter by date/location/person, and track its progress.

9 Registry Categories (Automatic Classification)

The AI automatically identifies and organizes tasks into these categories based on your existing paper register format:

1. Work Description कामाचे सविस्तर वर्णन - Detailed maintenance work descriptions
2. Site Updates साईट वरुन आपडेट घेणे - Updates received from various sites
3. Supervisor Watch सुपरवायझर/टेलिकॉलर वॉच - Supervisor and telecaller activity tracking
4. Worker Updates करस्तगण साईट आपडेट - Updates about workers at sites
5. Email Responses ई-मेल रिस्पाव - Email correspondence and responses
6. SOS/Consultant सल्लागार व घेर सेफिंग SOS - Emergency situations and consultant interactions
7. Site Visits साईट व्हिज़िट टीम - Team site visit records
8. Travel Records रेन रिकॉर्ड जाणे/येणे - Travel and transportation logs
9. Customer Communication नवीन/जुना कस्टमर सोबत कामासाठी बोलणे - New and existing customer interactions
